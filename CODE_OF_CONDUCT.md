# Code of Conduct

Tavo is committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free community.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat other people with respect and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Help make participation safe for people of every background and experience level.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban an account when necessary to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
